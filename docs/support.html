Simple Video Speed Controller Support

Simple Video Speed Controller changes the playback speed of HTML5 videos in Safari and other supported browsers.

Enable the Safari extension

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions, choose Simple Video Speed Controller, turn it on, and allow access to websites.

On Mac, open Safari > Settings > Extensions and enable Simple Video Speed Controller.

Troubleshooting

  • Reload video tabs that were already open when the extension was installed or updated.
  • Confirm that Safari has granted the extension access to the current website.
  • Some protected or custom video players may limit speed control.

Get help

Report a bug or request a feature on GitHub. Include your device, operating-system version, browser version, website, and steps to reproduce the problem.
