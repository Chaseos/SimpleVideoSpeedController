Simple Video Speed Controller Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Simple Video Speed Controller does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, browsing history, video content, or video metadata with the developer or third parties. It includes no developer-operated analytics, accounts, or servers.

Information stored on your device

The extension stores playback-speed preferences associated with website domains and the settings needed to provide its controls. This data is stored through the browser's extension storage. If browser sync is enabled, the browser provider may sync those preferences through your browser account. The developer does not receive that data.

Purchases

The Apple app offers optional consumable tips through Apple's StoreKit. Tips unlock nothing and create no account or persistent entitlement. Apple processes the purchase; the developer does not operate a receipt server or receive your payment details.

Your choices

You can clear stored extension preferences by removing the extension. You can also disable browser synchronization in your browser account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub.
