Simple Video Speed Controller

Control video playback speed on websites in Safari.

Set up once

In Settings, find Safari > Extensions. Turn on Simple Video Speed Controller and allow access to the websites where you watch video.

On newer versions of iOS and iPadOS, Safari is under Apps.

Enable the extension in Safari Settings, then allow it on the websites where you watch video.

The Safari extension is enabled.

The Safari extension is currently disabled.

Use it in Safari

  1. Open a website in Safari and start a video.
  2. Beside Safari’s address bar, tap the page menu on iPhone or the Extensions button on iPad, then select Simple Video Speed Controller.
  3. Choose a speed, or enter your own.

Works with website videos in Safari, not videos inside other apps.

To change a video's speed, open the extension in Safari’s toolbar while the video is playing.

Help

If the controls are missing or a speed change does not apply:

  • Check that the extension is enabled and allowed on the current website.
  • Reload the video tab after enabling or updating the extension.
  • Start the video before opening the controls.

Some video players limit speed control. Safari limits certain videos to 2× for smooth playback.

Privacy

Website access lets the extension find videos and change their playback speed. Your browsing history and video content are not sent to the developer.

Speed preferences and settings are stored through Safari’s extension storage. Your browser may sync them if browser sync is enabled.

There are no developer-operated accounts, analytics, or servers. Optional tips are handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment details.
